## Underlying factual sources used by the upstream dataset

The upstream source register distinguishes original Arabic question wording from factual data sources. Those sources include:

- Wikidata structured data: CC0 1.0.
- IANA HTTP status-code registry data: CC0 1.0.
- BIPM SI Brochure: CC BY 3.0 IGO; attribution is required.
- IUPAC periodic-table facts, UN statistical/member-state facts, and Quran Foundation chapter metadata: consult the upstream source register and the relevant source terms for the precise permitted reuse and attribution.

The upstream register is authoritative for the source mapping. Do not remove the per-question provenance fields or this notice when redistributing the imported questions.

## Existing questions not covered by the imported batch

The remaining questions already present in the project before this import have not been individually mapped to a third-party source or license in this notice. Their provenance must not be inferred from the MIT license above. Before commercial release, review the origin of those questions and retain only content that is original, public-domain, or used under a license/permission that permits the intended distribution and monetization.

## Google Play release checklist

- Keep this notice and required attribution in distributed source/content packages.
- Verify all question wording, explanations, images, fonts, audio, maps, and other assets separately; this notice covers only the imported question batch described above.
- Keep evidence of licenses and permissions available in case Google Play requests it.
- Do not describe the whole question bank or app as fully rights-cleared until the earlier questions and all other app assets have been audited.
